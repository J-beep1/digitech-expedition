3D FIELD PROTOTYPE — FULL CLIMB: BASE LEVEL 1 → 100
First-person, fully three-dimensional. Character models here are stylised placeholder geometry — green zombies, blocky survivors — standing in for the final imported models. The systems underneath (combat, base defence, inventory, building) don't depend on the art. As the Bastion grows, the world shifts through named regions (Logging Camp, Industrial Zone, Military Outpost, Abandoned City, Research Facility) with their own look, threats, and a boss encounter at each major milestone, ending with Patient Zero at Level 100.

Wall repairs auto-select your best material: 8 Stone gives a bigger +30% restore, 10 Wood gives +18% — stand near the fortress ring while it's damaged and press E. Bear Traps arm a moment after being placed and clamp the next zombie that walks over them, dealing damage and immobilising it. Built barricades are a separate, breakable defence you place anywhere — zombies that wander into one will stop and destroy it over time, so use them to hold a chokepoint rather than as a permanent wall. Base upgrades trigger the next raid immediately, so prepare first. Every firearm — including Patient Zero's Cannon — carries a scope: hold right-click to aim down sights for a steady 3x zoom, at the cost of turning slower.
